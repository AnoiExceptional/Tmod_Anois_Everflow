[h1]Anoi's Everflow[/h1]

Anoi's Everflow 专注于各具特色的复合职业体验。模组中的武器、护甲与召唤物不会被单一职业框定，而是通过近战与召唤、远程与魔法等组合，创造各自不同的战斗循环。我们希望每件装备不只是数值升级，而是拥有鲜明机制与成长空间，让玩家自由探索属于自己的混合流派。

[b]当前版本：0.3.2 公开测试版[/b]
欢迎反馈平衡性、多人游戏兼容性及其他游玩问题。

[h1]English[/h1]

Anoi's Everflow focuses on distinctive hybrid-class gameplay. Weapons, armor, and summons are not confined to a single class; instead, combinations such as melee and summoning or ranged and magic create their own combat loops. Each piece of equipment aims to offer more than a numerical upgrade, with a distinct mechanic and room to grow so players can discover their own mixed playstyles.

[b]Current version: 0.3.2 — Public Test[/b]
